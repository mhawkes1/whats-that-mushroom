Funga — the outline, round six

Eight outline mushrooms, each a single continuous path so there is no stroke hidden inside the mark — which is what made round five’s version blot at small sizes. Shown at 128, 60, 29 and 16 px, because an outline mark lives or dies small: stroke weight is absolute but the counters shrink with everything else. That is why “Drawn for 29” exists and is not the regular one reduced.
