Half Known — eight dissolves

Round one failed below 48 px because the stipple was a smooth gradient of tiny dots, and a smooth gradient is exactly what scaling destroys. Every one of these is discrete — a countable number of columns, blocks, bars or zones — so shrinking it loses detail instead of turning to noise. The fade also starts at about 36% across rather than 55%.
