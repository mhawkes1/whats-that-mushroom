Funga — logo, round five

Six developments of Cut Lengthways with the spore drops removed, then four plain mushrooms with no cut and no spores at all. Each at 128, 60 and 29 px, once inverted on a dark ground, and once locked up with the wordmark. The F variants are all one function with different numbers, so they stay a family rather than six unrelated drawings — whichever you pick, the others are a parameter away.

Round four's mark with the spore drops off. The cap is not untouched, though: dropping the spores made it read as a flag, so the rim is blunter and the underside tucks back under. Overhang is what says mushroom once nothing beneath it is doing the explaining.
