Funga — logo, round four

Six marks built on the Half Known idea you picked, redrawn for the new name. Each is shown at 128, 60 and 29 px, once on a dark ground because home screens have wallpaper, and once locked up with the wordmark — which is new, because a five-letter name can sit beside a mark and a twenty-character one could not. Nothing here uses a smooth gradient: every fade is a countable number of dots, bars or zones, which is what round one got wrong and what scaling punishes.
